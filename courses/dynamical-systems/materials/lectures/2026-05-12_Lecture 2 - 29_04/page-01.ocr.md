$m\ddot{X} = -KX$
this is not a dynamical system because there is redond deriv
a dynamical system is $\vec{X} = F(x)$

$\begin{cases} \dot{X} = V \\ m\dot{V} = -KX \end{cases}$
$\Downarrow$
this is a dynamical system
$\begin{cases} \dot{X} = V \\ \dot{V} = \frac{-KX}{m} \end{cases}$

energy of the system
$E = \frac{1}{2}mV^2 + \frac{1}{2}KX^2$

letr check that energy is conserved ($\dot{E}=0$)
$\dot{E} = m V \dot{V} + K X \dot{X} = m V \frac{-KX}{m} + K X V = -KXV + KXV = 0$

we can plot it
