# Fourier Properties

5. **Scaling/Shrinking:** $f(ax) \leftrightarrow \frac{1}{|a|} F\left(\frac{u}{a}\right)$

6. **Derivative:** $g(x) = \frac{d}{dx} f(x) \leftrightarrow G(u) = 2\pi i \, u \, F(u)$
   * $* G(0) = 0.$
   * $*$ Taking the derivative increases high frequencies (noise, edges)
   * $*$ Behaves like a high-pass filter (HPF).

7. **Rotation:** rotation of $f(x,y)$ by $\theta \leftrightarrow$ rotation of $F(u,v)$ by $\theta$ *(can be shown using polar coordinates)*

8. **Principle of Uncertainty:** No meaning to "a frequency at a point" !
   * every value $f(x)$ contributes to all frequencies $F(u)$
   * every frequency $F(u)$ contributes to all values $f(x)$
