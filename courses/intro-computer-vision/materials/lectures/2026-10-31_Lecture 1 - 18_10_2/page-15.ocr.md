# Complex Numbers

[diagram: An Argand diagram showing a complex number $Z = (a, b) = a + ib$ in the complex plane with real axis horizontally and imaginary axis vertically. The vector has magnitude $|Z|$ and makes an angle $\theta$ with the positive real axis. Its complex conjugate $\bar{Z} = (a, -b)$ is shown mirrored across the real axis at an angle $-\theta$ with magnitude $|Z|$.]

$$Z = \text{Re}(Z) + i\text{Im}(Z)$$
$$= a + ib$$
$$= |Z|e^{i\theta}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
&i = \sqrt{-1} \quad (i^2 = -1) \\
&e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta \quad (\text{a unit vector}) \\
&|Z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \quad (\text{Magnitude}) \\
&\theta = \text{tg}^{-1}(b/a) \quad (\text{phase}) \\
&\bar{Z} = Z^* = a - ib = |Z|e^{-i\theta} \quad (\text{conjugate})
\end{aligned}$$
