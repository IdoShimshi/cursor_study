# Convolution Properties

* Commutative: 
  $f * g = g * f$
* Associative: 
  $(f * g) * h = f * (g * h)$
* Homogeneous: 
  $f * (\lambda g) = \lambda f * g$
* Additive (Distributive): 
  $f * (g + h) = f * g + f * h$
* Shift-Invariant: 
  $f * g(x - x_0, y - y_0) = (f * g)(x - x_0, y - y_0)$
