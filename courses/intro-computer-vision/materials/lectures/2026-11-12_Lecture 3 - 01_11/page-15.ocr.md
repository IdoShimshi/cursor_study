Which projection model should I use?

* Perspective model is needed
    * In scenes that contain many depth differences
    * For accurate 3D reconstruction (stereo, structure from motion)
* Scaled orthographic can be used
    * When objects are small relative to their distance from the camera
    * Often sufficient for recognition applications
