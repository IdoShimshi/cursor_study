Camera Obscura (“dark room”)

• The origin of cameras started a long ago with camera obscura
• A camera obscura is a darkened room with a small hole at one side through which the image is projected onto a wall
• The principle of the camera obscura is called a pinhole camera model
• A simple camera, i.e., a light-proof box with a tiny aperture (pinhole)
• The size of the images depends on the distance between the object and the pinhole

First published picture of camera obscura.
Book *De Radio Astronomica et Geometrica* by Gemma Frisius, 1545.
