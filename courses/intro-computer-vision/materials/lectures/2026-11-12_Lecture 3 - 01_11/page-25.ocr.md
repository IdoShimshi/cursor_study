Lines and points in $\mathbb{P}^2$

Example. Determine the intersection of the lines $x = 1$ and $y = 1$

First we determine the homogeneous representation of the lines
$$x = 1 \rightarrow -1x + 1 = 0 \rightarrow \boldsymbol{l} = (-1, 0, 1)^T$$
$$y = 1 \rightarrow -1y + 1 = 0 \rightarrow \boldsymbol{l}' = (0, -1, 1)^T$$

$$\Rightarrow \boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{l} \times \boldsymbol{l}' = \begin{vmatrix} \boldsymbol{i} & \boldsymbol{j} & \boldsymbol{k} \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\Rightarrow$ inhomogeneous point $(1, 1)^T$
