Structure from Motion (SfM)
Challenges and applications

* The input to the problem is <u>only</u> the 2D images
* The 2D images are (perspective) <u>projections</u> of the 3D scene
* We need to move back <u>from the 2D world to the 3D world</u>
* <u>without</u> any prior knowledge on the 3D scene
* <u>without</u> knowledge on the locations of the cameras

<u>Applications</u>
* Navigation
* Autonomous driving
* Medical imaging, surgery guiding
* Augmented reality
* Photo organization
