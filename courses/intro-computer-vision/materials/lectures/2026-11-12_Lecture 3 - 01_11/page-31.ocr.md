Homography example

(A) Two cameras captured a world plane

[diagram: Two side-by-side camera view images of a checkered calibration pattern on a cluttered desk, with coordinate axes ranging from 200 to 1200/1800 pixels.]

* A set of points $U_i$ lying on the same plane is projected into two cameras
* $x_i \sim C_1 U_i$ and $y_i \sim C_2 U_i$ then there is a homography such that $x_i \propto H y_i$
* Calculate $H$, how many degrees of freedom?
