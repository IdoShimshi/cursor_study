# Orthographic projection
* Projection rays are (nearly) parallel
* Camera center at infinity
* When objects are far from the camera

[diagram: 3D coordinate system with X, Y, Z axes, a projection plane, and two human figures (one closer, one further) projected onto the plane. The projection rays are parallel to the Z-axis. Equations $x=X$ and $y=Y$ are shown.]

# Scaled orthographic

$x = sX$
$y = sY$
$s = \frac{f}{Z_0}$

* $Z_0$ is some typical distance of the scene
* Useful when the variance of the $Z$ values is small relative to the average depth, e.g., when the object is far from the camera

[diagram: 3D coordinate system with X, Y, Z axes, two projection planes at different Z distances, and two human figures (one closer, one further) projected onto their respective planes. The projection rays are parallel to the Z-axis.]
