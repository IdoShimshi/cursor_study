Homography: main points

$$\lambda \begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} h_{11} & h_{12} & h_{13} \\ h_{21} & h_{22} & h_{23} \\ h_{31} & h_{32} & h_{33} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

(A) If the scene is planar, then the images from any two cameras are related by a homography

(B) If camera rotates about its center, then the images are related by a homography irrespective of scene depth

Homography mapping is a $3 \times 3$ invertible matrix with 8 dof
