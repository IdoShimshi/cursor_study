# Ideal points (vanishing points)

* In the space $\mathbb{P}^2$ every pair of lines have a common intersection
* <u>Example.</u> Determine the intersection between the lines $x = 1$ and $x = 2$
$$x = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
* This homogeneous point does not have an inhomogeneous representation
* A vanishing point
* <u>Interpretation.</u> The point at infinity in the direction of $y$-axis
* The point $(0,1,0)^T$ lies on any line of the from $x = a$, represented as $(1,0,-a)$
