# A line in $\mathbb{P}^2$

* A projective line in the projective plane is a set of points
* A point in the projective plane is a ray through the origin
* A line = plane of rays through the origin
* The vector of coefficients $l$ (i.e., "the line $l$") is normal to the plane of rays
