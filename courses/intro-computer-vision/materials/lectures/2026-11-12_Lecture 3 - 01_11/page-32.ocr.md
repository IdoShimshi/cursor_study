* There are $9$ elements in $H$, however the scale does not matter ($H$ and $\lambda H$ represents the same projective transformation), so we are left with $8$ degrees of freedom.
* Now, how may equations do we need to solve $H$?

[diagram: Two images of a checkerboard setup with four highlighted points labeled $y_i$ on one image and $x_i$ on the other]

* Suppose, we know that $n$ points $y_i, i = 1, ..., n$ are transformed to $x_i$
* By the relation $x_i \propto H y_i$ each pair contributes $2$ equations
* So, in total we have $2n$ equations and $8$ degrees of freedom
  $$2n \geq 8 \iff n \geq 4$$
