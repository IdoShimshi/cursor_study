# Homogeneous coordinates

* In projective geometry we use homogeneous coordinates

* Adding a dimension, all points on a single ray are the same point, boil down to a single point in $\mathbb{R}^2$

* Enabling non-linear mappings to be represented by linear matrix equations
  * perspective projection
  * projective transformation

* Allowing representation of points at infinity
