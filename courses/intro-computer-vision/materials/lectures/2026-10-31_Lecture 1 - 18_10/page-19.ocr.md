# The Continuous Fourier Transform

**1D Continuous Fourier Transform:**

$$f(x) = \int_{u=-\infty}^{\infty} F(u)e^{+2\pi iux} du = \mathfrak{F}^{-1}(F(u))$$ **The Inverse Fourier Transform**

$$F(u) = \int_{x=-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)e^{-2\pi iux} dx = \mathfrak{F}(f(x))$$ **The Fourier Transform**

**2D Continuous Fourier Transform:**

$$f(x,y) = \int_{u=-\infty}^{\infty} \int_{v=-\infty}^{\infty} F(u,v)e^{+2\pi i(ux+vy)} dudv$$ **The Inverse Transform**

$$F(u,v) = \int_{x=-\infty}^{\infty} \int_{y=-\infty}^{\infty} f(x,y)e^{-2\pi i(ux+vy)} dxdy$$ **The Transform**
