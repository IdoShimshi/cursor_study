# Complex Numbers

[diagram: A complex plane with a horizontal "Real" axis and a vertical "Imaginary" axis. A vector $Z = (a, b)$ is shown in the first quadrant, making an angle $\theta$ with the real axis and having a magnitude $|Z|$. Its projection on the real axis is $a$ and on the imaginary axis is $b$. A second vector $\bar{Z} = (a, -b)$ is shown in the fourth quadrant, making an angle $-\theta$ with the real axis and having the same magnitude $|Z|$.]

$$Z = \text{Re}(Z) + i \text{Im}(Z)$$
$$= a + ib$$
$$= |Z|e^{i\theta}$$

$$i = \sqrt{-1} \quad (i^2 = -1)$$
$$e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta \quad \text{(a unit vector)}$$
$$|Z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \quad \text{(Magnitude)}$$
$$\theta = tg^{-1}(b/a) \quad \text{(phase)}$$
$$\bar{Z} = Z^* = a - ib = |Z|e^{-i\theta} \quad \text{(conjugate)}$$
