# The 1D Basis Functions $e^{2\pi iux}$

$$\mathbf{e^{2\pi iux} = \cos(2\pi ux) + i\sin(2\pi ux)}$$

$$\begin{array}{|l|}
\hline
\text{Re}(e^{2\pi iux}) = \cos(2\pi ux) \\
\text{Im}(e^{2\pi iux}) = \sin(2\pi ux) \\
\hline
\end{array}$$
