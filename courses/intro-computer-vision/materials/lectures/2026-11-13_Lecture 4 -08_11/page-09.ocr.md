# Projective geometry – vanishing (ideal) points

* Parallel lines intersect at ideal points
$$p = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
[diagram: A set of parallel lines in a plane converging towards points on a circle outside the plane.]
* Determined by the ratio $\frac{x}{y}$
* The complete set of ideal points is the line at infinity
* The line at infinity, $l_\infty$, is the $Z = 0$ plane
* $l_\infty = (0,0,1)^T$
[diagram: A 3D coordinate system with axes $x$, $y$, and $z$. A shaded plane is shown in the $xy$-plane where $z=0$.]
