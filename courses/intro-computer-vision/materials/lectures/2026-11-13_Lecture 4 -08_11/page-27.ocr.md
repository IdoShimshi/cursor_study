Projective reconstruction vs. Euclidean reconstruction
Uncalibrated reconstruction vs. Calibrated reconstruction

* Trying to build a 3D model from internet or photos taken by unknown cameras
* We do not know ahead of time the intrinsic calibration
* In such cases the reconstruction may look weird
* Orthogonal lines or planes in the world may not end up being reconstructed as orthogonal
* The distinction between calibrated camera and uncalibrated camera is tightly connected to the degrees of freedom of the reconstruction space
* Calibrated- up to similarity transformation
* Uncalibrated- up to projective transformation
* Nevertheless, in such situations we can still have the Epipolar Geometry between two views
