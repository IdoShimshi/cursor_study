# Epipolarity geometry – the Essential matrix

$$p = \frac{f}{Z} P, \quad q = \frac{f'}{Z'} Q, \quad Q = RP + t$$

$$t \times Q = t \times RP \quad (t \times t = 0)$$

$$0 = Q^T (t \times Q) = Q^T (t \times RP) = Q^T [t]_{\times} RP$$

$$0 = \frac{z' Z}{f' f} q^T [t]_{\times} Rp = q^T [t]_{\times} Rp$$

$$\mathbf{q^T E p = 0}$$

$$E = [t]_{\times} R \text{ is called the \textit{Essential} matrix}$$
