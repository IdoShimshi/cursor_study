# Epipolar geometry

- An **epipole** is the projection in one view of the camera center of the other view
- The epipole $e$ is the projection of $O'$ to the left image (using $C$)
- The epipole $e'$ is the projection of $O$ to the right image (using $C'$)
- $e, e'$ are usually outside the field of view
