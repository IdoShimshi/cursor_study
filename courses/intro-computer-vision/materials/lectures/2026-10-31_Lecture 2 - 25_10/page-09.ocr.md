The Convolution Theorem

$\mathcal{F}\{f(x)*g(x)\} = \mathcal{F}\{f(x)\}\cdot\mathcal{F}\{g(x)\}$

and similarly:
$\mathcal{F}\{f(x)g(x)\} = \mathcal{F}\{f(x)\}*\mathcal{F}\{g(x)\}$
