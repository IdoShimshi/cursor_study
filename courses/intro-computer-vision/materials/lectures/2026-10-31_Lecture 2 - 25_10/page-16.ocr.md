# Examples of $\underline{\textbf{Temporal}}$ Aliasing

[diagram: Image of a roof turbine with arrows indicating rotation. The turbine blades are numbered 1, 2, and 3, and arrows show their movement. An outer blue ellipse with a red arrow indicates the perceived rotation direction.]

What's going on...?!

* The turbine rotates fast $\underline{\text{to the left}}$.
* Each blade moves almost to the next blade position between frames.
    $\square$ So, we wrongly perceive rotation of the blades $\underline{\text{to the right}}$
    $\square$ $\underline{\text{TEMPORAL ALIASING!}}$
It happens because the temporal sampling rate (framerate) is too slow.
