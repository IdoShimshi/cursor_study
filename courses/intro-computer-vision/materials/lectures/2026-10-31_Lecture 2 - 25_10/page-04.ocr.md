# Convolution Properties

* Commutative:
  $f*g = g*f$
* Associative:
  $(f*g)*h = f*(g*h)$
* Homogeneous:
  $f*(\lambda g) = \lambda f*g$
* Additive (Distributive):
  $f*(g+h) = f*g+f*h$
* Shift-Invariant
  $f*g(x-x0,y-y0) = (f*g) (x-x0,y-y0)$
