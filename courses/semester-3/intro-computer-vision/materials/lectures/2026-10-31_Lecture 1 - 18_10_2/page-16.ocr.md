The 1D Basis Functions $e^{2\pi iux}$

$$e^{2\pi iux} = \cos(2\piux) + i\sin(2\piux)$$

$$\text{Re}(e^{2\pi iux}) = \cos(2\piux)$$
$$\text{Im}(e^{2\pi iux}) = \sin(2\piux)$$

[diagram: A plot of $\cos(2\piux)$ as a function of $x$. The wave oscillates between $-1$ and $1$, crossing the x-axis multiple times. A red double-ended arrow indicates the wavelength, labeled $1/u$, spanning one full period.]

$-$ The wavelength is $1/u$.
$-$ The frequency is $u$.
