Pinhole camera model

* Pinhole is useful as a mathematical model
* The mapping from three dimensions onto two dimensions is called *perspective projection*
* Perspective: “perspicere” (latine) – to see through

[diagram: A 3D illustration of a pinhole camera. Light rays from a candle pass through a pinhole to form an inverted image on an image plane inside a box. A dashed "virtual image" plane is shown in front of the pinhole, where the image would appear upright.]

Pinhole camera - focal length

* The depth of the pinhole camera is called the *focal length*.
* The longer the focal length, the narrower the angle of view and the higher the magnification.
* The shorter the focal length, the wider the angle of view and the lower the magnification.

[diagram: A cross-section of a pinhole camera showing the focal length as the distance from the pinhole to the film or sensor. Below, two diagrams compare a short focal length with a wide angle of view versus a long focal length with a narrow angle of view.]
