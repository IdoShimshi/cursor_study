# Pinhole camera

* The <ins>ideal</ins> pinhole camera would have an infinitely small hole, yielding dark image

* Pinhole too big – blurry image
[diagram: Two blurry black and white images of text "LUZ OPTICA FOTOGRAFIA". The left image is labeled "2 mm" and is very blurry. The right image is labeled "1 mm" and is slightly less blurry.]

* Pinhole is smaller- sharper but dimmer
[diagram: Two sharper black and white images of text "LUZ OPTICA FOTOGRAFIA". The left image is labeled "0.6mm" and is relatively sharp. The right image is labeled "0.35 mm" and is the sharpest of the set.]

* Pinhole too small – sharpness is lost due to diffraction
[diagram: Two blurry black and white images of text "LUZ OPTICA FOTOGRAFIA". The left image is labeled "0.15 mm" and shows some blurring. The right image is labeled "0.07 mm" and is very blurry and dim.]
