# Examples of Temporal Aliasing

[diagram: A photograph of a roof ventilator turbine with overlaid colored arrows and a curved blue arrow showing direction of rotation, accompanied by the text "What's going on...?!"]

* The turbine rotates fast $\underline{\text{to the left}}$.
* Each blade moves almost to the next blade position between frames.
$\implies$ So, we wrongly perceive rotation of the blades $\underline{\text{to the right}}$
$\implies$ $\textbf{\textit{\color{red}TEMPORAL ALIASING!}}$
It happens because the temporal sampling rate (framerate) is too slow.
