# Projective plane - points

* A point $p \in \mathbb{P}^2$ is a (two sided) ray in $\mathbb{R}^3$

$$p = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha x \\ \alpha y \\ \alpha \end{pmatrix} \alpha \neq 0$$

* $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^2$
