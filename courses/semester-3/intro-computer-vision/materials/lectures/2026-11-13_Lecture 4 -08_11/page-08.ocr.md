# Projective geometry – lines

* A point $p \in \mathbb{P}^2 \iff$ a (two sided) ray in $\mathbb{R}^3$
$$\alpha p \text{ with } \alpha \neq 0$$
* A line $l \in \mathbb{P}^2 \iff$ a central plane in $\mathbb{R}^3$
* With representation $l = k(a, b, c)^T, k \neq 0$
* A point $p \in \mathbb{P}^2$ resides on a line $l \in \mathbb{P}^2 \iff l^T p = 0$
