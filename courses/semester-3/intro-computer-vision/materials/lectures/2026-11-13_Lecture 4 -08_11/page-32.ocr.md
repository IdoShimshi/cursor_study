# Epipolar geometry

[diagram: Two diagrams illustrating epipolar geometry. The left diagram shows two image planes, a baseline connecting the two camera centers, an epipolar plane $\pi$, and epipolar lines $l, l'$ with epipoles $e, e'$. The right diagram shows corresponding points and lines in the two views.]

* The **epipolar planes** rotate about the baseline
* Any **epipolar plane $\pi$** intersects the image plane at **epipolar lines $l, l'$**
* The camera center resides on any ray which is projected to the other view. Therefore, the projection of the camera center (**epipole**) should reside on each **epipolar line**
* The **camera baseline** intersects each image plane at the **epipoles $e, e'$**
* All **epipolar lines intersect at the epipole**
