# Epipolar geometry -- the Essential matrix

The calibrated case

* $Q = RP + t, \quad p = \frac{f}{z}P, \quad q = \frac{f'}{z'}Q$
* The Essential matrix $E = [t]_{\times}R$
* satisfies $q^T Ep = 0$

$$[t]_{\times} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -t_3 & t_2 \\ t_3 & 0 & -t_1 \\ -t_2 & t_1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
